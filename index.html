Nigeria's SEC Drafts a Residency Test for Every Exchange That Wants Its Users. The ARIP Count Is Finally Resolved.

Nigeria's SEC published draft prudential rules on 20 August 2026 requiring local incorporation, a resident CEO and up to $1.49 million in capital from crypto exchanges serving Nigerians — moving ahead of the Executive Order's own coordination framework, still unconfirmed roughly ten days past deadline. This cycle also resolves, by reconciliation rather than assumption, the ARIP cohort-size discrepancy flagged last issue: 14 firms, confirmed.

Methodology — Qualitative Status, Not a Numeric Index

A source-verification audit in Vol. 20 found several of these — including a named Rwandan licensing window, a Moroccan licensee "duopoly" claim, and the numeric scores themselves — could not be traced to a verifiable source, and retracted them.
