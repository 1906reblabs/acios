Vol. 24 — 16 September 2026 — Kenya's Licence Clock Now Reads Forty-Nine Days.

Latest ⚠ Kenya: 49 Days to Deadline, Zero Licensees ★ Ghana: Sandbox Cohort 2 Confirms Licensing Waits Until 2027 ⚠ Nigeria: SEC Silent 13 Days 16 September 2026
Kenya’s Licence Clock Now Reads Forty-Nine Days. Zero Operators Are Licensed, and No One Has Said What Happens Next.

Kenya's VASP Regulations, 2026 have been in force since 22 July, with a statutory compliance deadline of 4 November for every pre-existing operator. That date is now 49 days away, and this cycle's research found no confirmed licensee and no announced grace period. Separately, ACIOS updates its Ghana coverage: Yellow Card's 4 September admission into the second cohort of Ghana's VASP sandbox confirms, on the SEC's own published sequencing, that full licensing outside the sandbox will not open before the twelve-month pilot concludes — pointing to early 2027. Nigeria's SEC has gone thirteen days without a statement since its 3 September comment-period close, and the Harmonised Implementation Framework passes 31 days overdue. South Africa's two tracks and Zimbabwe, Morocco, Rwanda, WAEMU and Flutterwave carry no material change.

  • Kenya's 4 November VASP compliance deadline is now 49 days away with zero confirmed licensees located and no grace period announced
  • The deadline is fixed by statute (Section 47 of the VASP Act), not regulator discretion — a genuinely novel test case in ACIOS's tracked record
  • Ghana's Yellow Card admitted to sandbox Cohort 2 (4 Sep); full licensing confirmed gated behind the 12-month pilot's conclusion, ~early 2027
  • Nigeria's SEC silence extends to 13 days since the 3 Sep comment close; Harmonised Framework now 31 days overdue
  • South Africa's SARB cross-border Crypto Asset Manual comment window closes in 14 days; SCA appeal remains undated
  • Two new predictions, P-24-01 and P-24-02, logged to prediction memory
